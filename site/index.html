    How the power score works

    Not just the standings. Every team gets a score from 0 to 100, mixing:

      Each part is scaled so the best team in the league scores 100 and the worst 0. Arrows show the move since last week. All-play is your record if you played all 11 teams every week. Luck is real wins minus all-play wins. Scores come from Sleeper's official season totals (exact from week 4; weeks 1-3 of 2026-27 are estimates matched to those totals). 🚨 FRAUD = at least about one more win than the team's scoring has earned; 😤 ROBBED = at least about one win fewer (both from week 3 on).

      🎟️ Playoff odds: EquipoGPT plays out the rest of the season 10,000 times with the Matchup Odds projections (allowing for not knowing exactly how good each team is, less so as the season goes on). Top 6 make the playoffs, top 2 get a bye, ties go to points scored. Title = winning the whole bracket.

        Trade Report Cards

        How trades are graded

        Each side gets the fantasy points its new players scored in that team's starting lineup (bench weeks don't count, just like in a real matchup), from the week they arrived until they left (traded, dropped, or still there 🔒). A draft pick is worth the lineup points of the player taken with it. The grade compares each side's share of the points with an even split.

        Not graded yet: trades with future picks (⏳ waiting on the draft), trades less than 4 weeks old, and tiny trades where under 200 points changed hands. "↪ moved on" means the player left before playing a week for them.

          Matchup Odds

          How the odds are made

          Each team's projected score mixes two things, in the proportion that predicted past Das League games best: 30% its 10 best players who actually have a game this week, each at his usual weekly score, plus 60% of the next 5 (bench depth: in a lock-in league a bench player with an early game can be swapped in and locked), and 70% the team's real points per week so far. In Das League a player's week counts one game, so how many games he gets matters little, but having none (or being hurt) matters a lot.

          🩼 Players Sleeper lists as Out or on IR (or sitting in an IR slot) are left out. Weekly scores swing about points, so the bigger the projected gap, the bigger the favourite.

          Moneyline: -150 means bet 150 to win 100 (favourite); +130 means bet 100 to win 130 (underdog). Spread: the favourite has to win by more than this. Total: both teams' points added together, over or under.

          Opening lines are set before the week starts. Live lines use the current scores straight from Sleeper, blended with the projection: early in the week they lean on the projection, by Sunday on the score itself.

          The season record re-runs the model before each finished week, using only what was known then.

          Lock-in Report

            How it's worked out

            Every week each starter counts one game: the one you lock (or his last game if you don't). For every starter in every week, EquipoGPT compares the game that counted with his average game that week. Add that up over your 10 starters and you get your lock edge: points a week gained (or lost) by when you locked.

            Best-game rate: how often, when a player had 2+ games, the best one was the one that counted. Regular seasons from finished years only, where Sleeper's saved scores are exact.
