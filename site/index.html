        Trade Report Cards

        How trades are graded

        Each side gets the fantasy points its new players scored for that team, from the week they arrived until they left (traded, dropped, or still there 🔒). A draft pick is worth the points of the player taken with it. The grade compares each side's share of the points with an even split.

        Not graded yet: trades with future picks (⏳ waiting on the draft), trades less than 4 weeks old, and tiny trades where under 200 points changed hands. "↪ moved on" means the player left before playing a week for them.

          Matchup Odds

          EquipoGPT's lines for every matchup. Just for fun: nobody is taking bets (except maybe Jacobo).

          How the odds are made

          Each team's projected score = 60% its points per week this season + 40% the points per week of the 10 best players on its roster right now. Weekly scores swing about points around a team's average, so the bigger the projected gap, the bigger the favourite.

          Moneyline: -150 means bet 150 to win 100 (favourite); +130 means bet 100 to win 130 (underdog). Spread: the favourite has to win by more than this. Total: both teams' points added together, over or under.

          Opening lines are set before the week starts. Live lines use the current scores straight from Sleeper: the lead now plus the projected scoring still to come, with less uncertainty as the week runs out.

          The season record re-runs the model before each finished week, using only what was known then.
